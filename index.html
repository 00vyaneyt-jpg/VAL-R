Community Rules

01 — Respect

Treat members and staff with respect. No harassment, discrimination or targeted abuse.

02 — No spam

Avoid spam, flooding, excessive mentions and disruptive behavior.

03 — No cheating

Do not promote exploits, cheating or unfair gameplay in the community.

04 — Keep it appropriate

Keep content suitable for the community and follow Discord's rules.

05 — Staff decisions

Follow reasonable staff instructions and use the appropriate channel for questions.

06 — Privacy

Do not share another person's private information or credentials.
