Rules

Keep VALØR welcoming, respectful, and enjoyable for everyone.

01 · Respect

Treat members and staff with respect. No harassment, bullying, or targeted abuse.

02 · No Spam

Avoid spam, excessive messages, disruptive behavior, or unwanted mentions.

03 · No Illegal Content

Do not share illegal, malicious, or dangerous content through VALØR.

04 · Keep It Appropriate

Keep conversations and shared content suitable for the community.

05 · No Exploiting

Do not abuse bugs, exploits, or systems in VALØR projects.

06 · Staff Decisions

Follow reasonable staff instructions. Appeals can be handled through the appropriate VALØR channel.

07 · Protect Privacy

Never share another person's private information without permission.

08 · Use Common Sense

If something could seriously harm the community or its members, don't do it.
